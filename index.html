The Grandeur
Within

Winging in the hollows,
closer to the sky

look up, if you ever happen to stand beneath
the dome,
and you will know why I found its captives,
with each brush with passing time it is
much worse —

no scene the sky visits is ever quite the same.
How I could wade through the deep and
tender blue,
as clouds drift by, and the miracles pool, still
and tame.

and miracles pool,
still and tame.
